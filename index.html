DESTINATION GUIDE

Find your kind of Sri Lanka

Explore popular regions and build a route that suits your time. Distances and seasonal conditions can affect the best itinerary.

HERITAGE

Sigiriya & Dambulla

Climb the iconic rock fortress, explore ancient frescoes and visit Dambulla’s cave temples. Go early to beat the heat.

CULTURE

Kandy

Visit the Temple of the Tooth, walk by the lake and discover traditional arts, gardens and hill-country culture.

ANCIENT CITIES

Anuradhapura & Polonnaruwa

Explore sacred stupas, stone carvings, royal ruins and centuries of Sri Lankan history.

HILL COUNTRY

Nuwara Eliya & Tea Country

See emerald tea fields, cool-weather gardens, waterfalls and colonial-era architecture.

SCENIC ESCAPES

Ella & Haputale

Enjoy mountain viewpoints, the Nine Arch Bridge, tea trails and hikes such as Little Adam’s Peak.

BEACHES

Galle, Unawatuna & the South

Wander Galle Fort, relax on sandy beaches and discover coastal cafés, swimming spots and sunset views.

CITY BREAK

Colombo

Explore Galle Face Green, the Fort and Pettah districts, temples, museums, shopping and Sri Lankan food.

EAST COAST

Nilaveli & Trincomalee

Enjoy wide beaches, boat trips and seasonal snorkelling or diving near Pigeon Island when conditions permit.

UNDERWATER

Diving & Snorkelling

Discover reefs and marine life with a qualified local operator. Suitable sites depend on season, weather and experience.

MARINE LIFE

Whale & Dolphin Watching

Join a responsible, licensed boat operator when seasonal conditions are suitable. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

WILDLIFE

Yala, Udawalawe & Wilpattu

Look for elephants, birds, crocodiles and other wildlife on a safari with an authorised park guide.

MORE TO EXPLORE

Jaffna, Mannar, Kalpitiya & the East

Discover northern culture, island landscapes, lagoons, kite-surfing and quieter coastal towns.

This is a curated guide to major regions, not a literal list of every attraction. Ask us to add places to your custom route.

MAKE MEMORIES

Things you can do in Sri Lanka

Mix relaxing days with outdoor adventure, local culture and wildlife.

Safari jeep adventures

Explore national parks in a safari jeep with a qualified guide. Park entry, jeep and guide fees should be confirmed in your quote.

Diving & snorkelling

Meet colourful reef life with a reputable dive centre. Equipment, certification and sea conditions vary by trip.

Nilaveli beach days

Unwind on the east coast, swim where safe and ask about boat trips to Pigeon Island during suitable seasons.

Whale watching

Plan a sea excursion with a responsible operator. Trips depend on season and weather, and sightings cannot be promised.

Hiking & scenic trains

Walk to hill-country viewpoints and enjoy a memorable train journey through tea plantations and mountain scenery.

Food, tea & local culture

Try rice and curry, hoppers, tropical fruit, tea tastings, craft markets and cultural performances.
