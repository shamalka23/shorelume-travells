DESTINATION GUIDE

Find your kind of Sri Lanka

Explore popular regions and build a route that suits your time. Distances and seasonal conditions can affect the best itinerary.

HERITAGE

Sigiriya & Dambulla

Climb the iconic rock fortress, explore ancient frescoes and visit Dambulla’s cave temples.

CULTURE

Kandy

Visit the Temple of the Tooth, walk by the lake and discover traditional arts and gardens.

ANCIENT CITIES

Anuradhapura & Polonnaruwa

Explore sacred stupas, stone carvings, royal ruins and centuries of Sri Lankan history.

HILL COUNTRY

Nuwara Eliya & Tea Country

Discover emerald tea fields, cool-weather gardens, waterfalls and beautiful mountain scenery.

SCENIC ESCAPES

Ella & Haputale

Enjoy mountain viewpoints, the Nine Arch Bridge, tea trails and scenic hiking routes.

BEACHES & HERITAGE

Galle, Unawatuna & the South

Explore Galle Fort, relax on sandy beaches and enjoy coastal cafés and sunset views.

CITY BREAK

Colombo

Explore Galle Face Green, historic districts, temples, museums, shopping and local food.

EAST COAST

Nilaveli & Trincomalee

Enjoy beautiful beaches, boat trips and seasonal snorkelling near Pigeon Island.

MARINE LIFE

Dolphin Watching

Experience the excitement of spotting dolphins in Sri Lanka’s beautiful waters with a local boat operator. Sightings depend on the season and sea conditions.

WILDLIFE

Yala, Udawalawe & Wilpattu

Look for elephants, birds and other wildlife on a safari with an authorised park guide.

NORTHERN SRI LANKA

Jaffna & the North

Discover northern culture, historic sites, local cuisine and unique coastal landscapes.

Explore these destinations and ask us to create a personalised Sri Lanka itinerary.

MAKE MEMORIES

Things you can do in Sri Lanka

Mix relaxing days with outdoor adventure, local culture and wildlife.

Safari jeep adventures

Explore national parks in a safari jeep with a qualified guide. Park entry, jeep and guide fees should be confirmed in your quote.

Diving & snorkelling

Meet colourful reef life with a reputable dive centre. Equipment, certification and sea conditions vary by trip.

Nilaveli beach days

Unwind on the east coast, swim where safe and ask about boat trips to Pigeon Island during suitable seasons.

Whale watching

Plan a sea excursion with a responsible operator. Trips depend on season and weather, and sightings cannot be promised.

Scenic trains

Enjoy a memorable train journey through tea plantations and mountain scenery.

Food, tea & local culture

Try rice and curry, hoppers, tropical fruit, tea tastings, craft markets and cultural performances.
